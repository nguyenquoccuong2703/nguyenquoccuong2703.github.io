Rewarded and offerwall UA: which events to track

How to design the in-game events that rewarded, offerwall and playtime networks pay on.

Rewarded and offerwall networks pay users to install and play, so they need in-game events to reward and to bill on. The quality of these campaigns depends heavily on picking the right events.

Events almost every network needs

  • App opened, to confirm the user is active.
  • Ad revenue, for ROAS-based campaigns in ad-monetized games.
  • Purchase, for games with IAP. Track at least two packs: an entry-level pack and a high-value pack of around $15.
  • Level or stage completed, as complete_level_X or complete_stage_X.

Choosing level milestones

Networks reward users at checkpoints, so pick levels by how long it takes a typical player to reach them, not by round numbers.

  • Spread milestones from day 1 out to day 30 or beyond, for example around days 1, 3, 7, 14, 21, 30 and later.
  • Mix early, mid and late milestones. A common guide is several events completed within the first week, several in the second week and several after it.
  • The first event should be easy, with a very high completion rate. The final event should be reached by only a small share of users.
  • Some networks also accept repeatable events such as every N ads watched.

Pricing models you will meet

  • CPI: a fixed price per install, often with a minimum by country.
  • CPE: payment per completed event, which suits multi-reward offers.
  • ROAS-based: the network optimizes toward revenue, which needs reliable ad revenue and purchase events.
  • Playtime-based: users are rewarded for minutes played plus milestones.

Before you launch

  • Check each network's strongest countries and inventory type.
  • Ask the account manager for their recommended bid and reward structure.
  • Prefer networks with fraud protection or identity checks if you need high-quality traffic.
  • Compare cohorts against your other channels on retention and ROAS, not just CPI.
