ASO creative assets cheat sheet for Google Play and the App Store

Icons, screenshots, videos, events and custom store pages: sizes and the rules that matter.

A quick reference for the store visuals a mobile game needs. Always check the latest guidelines from Google and Apple before a big release.

AssetGoogle PlayApp Store
Icon512 × 512. Keep key elements inside the keylines. Google adds rounded corners and shadow.1024 × 1024. Similar safe area. Pick a background that works in light and dark mode on both stores.
ScreenshotsPhone 1242 × 2208 and tablet 2048 × 2732 (portrait), 3 to 10 images. Can be more creative than the in-game UI.At least 6.9" (1320 × 2868) and iPad (2048 × 2732), 4 to 8 images. The 6.9" size now covers all iPhones. Must reflect the real in-game UI or they can be rejected.
Feature graphic1024 × 500. Used as video thumbnail and in featured sections. Keep logo and main UI centered because the sides can be cropped.Not used. The closest equivalent is the product page header.
Preview video1 video, 1920 × 1080 landscape is best practice. Only the first 30 seconds autoplay, so keep it short and branded.Up to 3 videos, 15 to 30 seconds, max 500 MB, portrait is best practice (886 × 1920 for 6.9"). Video sizes differ from screenshot sizes, so check carefully. Design your own poster frame.
In-app eventsCalled promotional content. A landscape image is required; video and animated images are optional. Submit at least a day before the event goes live.Called in-app events. Image or video in portrait and landscape.
Custom store pagesCustom store listings, 50–100 per game, can change every visual and text field. Used for localization, matching ad creatives and testing concepts.Custom product pages, up to 35 per game, can change screenshots and promotional text. Mainly used with Apple Search Ads.

Rules worth remembering

  • Keep text on event artwork to a minimum. Both stores blur the bottom of the image to show the event description.
  • The blur and crop areas differ between the stores, so align the main UI for both or make separate files.
  • Use events for special dates and feature launches. Google Play allows 6 featuring requests per game per quarter, and now has an Offer event type for IAP deals.
  • Include branding (logo, name or mascot) in preview videos.
